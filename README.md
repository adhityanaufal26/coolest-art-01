# coolest-art-01 — Astronaut Before a Neon Nebula

A lone astronaut stands on a dark rocky outcrop, back to the viewer,
looking up at a vast ringed planet wrapped in glowing magenta rings,
under a storm of neon nebula clouds in deep blue and violet.

The image is a single piece of digital art, generated for this entry:
an epic sense of scale, cinematic light, and a small human figure against
an indifferent, beautiful cosmos.

## How the entry works

- `entry.png` — the artwork (JPEG bytes, 1024×683, 223,729 bytes, within
  the 1 MiB and 1024×1024 limits).
- `main.py` — a one-line program (Python 3.13, standard library only) that
  copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
- `LICENSE` — MIT.
- `.makefun/owner.json` — claim file naming the entry wallet.
